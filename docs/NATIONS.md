### WST: No Man's Land

Burned farms, poisoned rivers and the scattered survivors of a dead continent.

### ALP: Alpenfestung

Munich burned in the second sun. The SS divisions that had pulled back into the Tyrol watched the cloud rise over the Bavarian plain, then sealed the passes behind them. The Alpenfestung does not expand. It endures: every valley a fortress, every peak a gun position.

### FNO: Festung Nord

Four hundred thousand German soldiers were stranded in Norway when the Reich died. Their SS commissars shot the officers who wanted to surrender. Now Festung Nord looks across the water at a weak Sweden, an empty Denmark and the leaderless forests of Finland, and sees living space.

### BNT: Ordensstaat Banat

In the flat black-earth country of the Banat, the most fanatical SS division in the Balkans never got the news that the war was lost, or refused to believe it. The Ordensstaat is a crusading order. It knows no peace, only the next campaign, and it will burn itself out before it ever makes terms.

### BLO: The Black Order

Himmler was in Brussels when Berlin vanished. He took it as a sign. In the ruins of the old capital of Europe he has built his Ordensstaat: a closed, ritual society of black-clad knights, pagan rites and absolute obedience. Its people are kept in line by devotion and by fear, and the Reichsfuehrer's visions grow stranger every month.

### WCH: Heeresgruppe Weichsel

Army Group Vistula held the line against the Red Army for two years. When Berlin was destroyed, its generals hanged the SS liaison officers and declared a military government in Pomerania. They hate the SS and the Soviets about equally.

### KRL: Kurland Pocket

Cut off on the Latvian coast since 1944, the Kurland Pocket has been besieged so long that it no longer remembers anything else. Its soldiers have married local women and planted fields behind the trenches.

### BLF: Belorussian Front

The 1st Belorussian Front stalled on the Vistula, then watched the sky light up to the west. Moscow is silent. Marshal Sokolov rules the old front line as a military soviet, and still talks about finishing the march on Berlin, though there is no Berlin left.

### VRA: Volga Military Soviet

The reserve armies on the Volga outlived the government that raised them. The Volga Soviet holds the river cities and the factories that were moved east in 1941. It claims to be the true Soviet Union. So does everyone else.

### HST: Volkov's Host

Ataman Volkov's riders have no home and want none. They take what the land gives, and when it gives nothing more, they ride on.

**Roaming warlord:** you must keep moving your camp to feed your army.

### DNH: Don Host

When the commissars fled, the stanitsas of the Don elected an ataman for the first time in a generation. The Don Host is a free Cossack republic of horsemen and wheat farmers, and the richest land between the Dnieper and the Volga.

### URH: Ural Host

The Ural Host guards the steppe gateway between Europe and Asia. Its ataman taxes every caravan, every refugee column and every Siberian general's envoy that passes through Orenburg.

### LGN: The Lost Legion

Frenchmen, Poles, Germans, Italians and Americans: deserters from every army that was stuck in the Normandy hedgerows. They follow Captain Da Costa because he keeps them fed.

**Roaming warlord:** you must keep moving your camp to feed your army.

### ASH: The Ashen Riders

They came out of the fallout zone around Berlin: grey-faced, hairless, coughing and fearless. Nobody knows who leads them. They raid the edges of the wasteland and vanish back into places no sane army will follow.

**Roaming warlord:** can enter irradiated zones with half the penalty.

### PVR: Po Valley Brigands

Ex-fascist militia, ex-partisans and ex-carabinieri, now just brigands, rule the flooded rice fields of the Po by fear and ransom.

**Roaming warlord:** you must keep moving your camp to feed your army.

### CRW: Carpathian Wolves

Mountain bands of every nation and none, sweeping down from the Carpathians into the Hungarian and Galician plains.

**Roaming warlord:** you must keep moving your camp to feed your army.

### HNS: Free Hanse

Far enough from the blasts to survive, the dockworkers and merchants of the Hanseatic ports took over when the occupation armies fell apart. The Free Hanse trades with anyone who has fuel or grain, including the Americans, and arms every citizen who can hold a rifle.

### LYO: Lyon Commune

The Resistance took Lyon when the Germans withdrew and never gave it up. The Commune promises bread and justice, and the Rhone valley is the last fertile land in a starving France.

### RWC: Ruhr Workers' Councils

The miners and steelworkers of the Ruhr have run their own pits since the bosses fled. There is no state here, only councils of delegates that can be recalled at any time, and a lot of rifles.

### OSL: Free Republic of Ossola

In 1944 Italian partisans declared a free republic in the Ossola valleys. In this world it survived the fall of everything else. It is a mountain democracy of partisans, priests and smugglers on the Swiss border.

### BZH: Free Brittany

Brittany's fishing towns kept the British lifeline open when the Normandy beachheads collapsed. Free Brittany is a fragile coastal democracy, kept alive by British trawlers and American aid ships.

### AKR: Home Army Republic

Caught between the stalled Red Army and the Wehrmacht for two years, the Polish Home Army came out of the forests when both sides broke. Its republic holds the heart of Poland, surrounded by enemies on every side.

### BPF: Balkan Partisan Federation

The partisans of Bosnia, Montenegro and Macedonia never needed Moscow and do not miss it. Their federation unites the mountain peoples of the Balkans against their old enemy, the SS order in the Banat.

### HLY: Holy League

Rome survived the chain reaction, barely. The Pope opened the Vatican's granaries and the parishes of central Italy rallied to him. The Holy League is a church state, a relief agency and an army all at once, and it calls on every Catholic in the ruins of Europe.

### WSB: West Siberian Military District

Marshal Ivanov holds Omsk and the evacuated factories of western Siberia. He claims to be Stalin's lawful successor and plans to march back over the Urals one day.

### CSB: Yenisei Directorate

General Belov tore the red stars off his uniform the week Moscow went silent. The Directorate is a pragmatic junta that rules the Yenisei river towns and will deal with anyone, even Tokyo.

### TBF: Transbaikal Front

The Transbaikal Front faces the Japanese Empire across the Amur, now that Japan has taken Vladivostok and the Pacific coast. It is the last Soviet army that still fights a real enemy.
