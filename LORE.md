# Shattered Continent: Lore Bible

The canonical record of the setting. Add to it as decisions are made. Items marked **[OPEN]** are still undecided.

---

## Point of divergence

| When | What happened |
|---|---|
| **June–Aug 1944** | D-Day stalls. The Allies are pinned in the Normandy hedgerows and never break out. |
| **Late 1944** | The Soviet summer offensive runs out of steam on the Vistula, in Poland. |
| **1945** | Two years of stalemate on both fronts. Washington, desperate to end the European war, uses its first atomic bombs on **Berlin and Munich** instead of Japan. |
| **1945** | **The chain reaction**: the bombings set off a cascade of collapse across Europe. **[OPEN]** What exactly was it? Ideas: firestorms and fallout carried by the wind; German retaliation with dirty bombs or chemical stockpiles; a total breakdown of supply that turned into famine and plague; or an exaggerated "atmospheric" effect from the early bombs. |
| **1945–46** | Every government in Europe collapses. Armies are cut off from their capitals and turn into states of their own. |
| **March 1946** | **Game start.** |

## The world in 1946

### The great powers
- **United States**: the only nuclear power. The bombings succeeded and horrified the world. **[OPEN]** Is the US still at war with Japan, in an armistice, or in a cold war? How does guilt over Europe shape its politics?
- **Japanese Empire**: never bombed, never defeated. It has taken **Vladivostok and the Soviet Pacific coast** and dominates East Asia.
- **Republic of China**: slowly growing in strength. **[OPEN]** Still at war with Japan? Are the Chinese communists still around?

### Britain and Ireland
- **United Kingdom**: survived and is *somewhat* stable, but faces an **Irish rising** in Ulster and **socialist uprisings** (Red Clydeside, spreading to Wales and the North).
- **Ireland**: sees its chance to take the North.

### Spared nations
Switzerland, Sweden, Spain, Portugal and Turkey's Thrace survived intact (`sc_is_spared_state`). **[OPEN]** Should any of them fall too?

### Europe: the ruins
**No Man's Land** covers most of the continent: dangerous, depopulated wasteland. The **fallout zones** around Berlin and Munich are deadly.

#### SS holdouts
| Nation | Where | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Alpenfestung** | Tyrol / Salzburg | **Defensive.** Sealed itself in the Alps after Munich burned. Endures rather than expands. |
| **Festung Nord** | Norway | **Expansionist.** 400,000 stranded troops Norway can't feed. Eyes Sweden, Denmark and Finland. |
| **Ordensstaat Banat** | Banat / Vojvodina | **Fanatical.** A crusading order that knows no peace and decays without war. Enemy of the Balkan partisans. |
| **The Black Order** | Brussels / Belgium | **Cult.** Led by **Heinrich Himmler**, who was in Brussels when Berlin vanished and took it as a sign. A closed ritual society that runs on devotion and fear. |

#### Wehrmacht remnants
- **Heeresgruppe Weichsel** (Pomerania): regular army generals who hanged their SS minders. Hate the SS and the Soviets equally.
- **Kurland Pocket** (Latvia): besieged since 1944. Half settled, half still in the trenches.

#### Red Army remnants
- **Belorussian Front** (eastern Poland / Belarus): the army that stalled on the Vistula. Its marshal still dreams of marching on Berlin.
- **Volga Military Soviet** (Kazan–Samara–Saratov): reserve armies and evacuated factories. Claims to be the real USSR.

#### Cossacks
- **Volkov's Host**: nomadic cavalry roaming Ukraine and Poland.
- **Don Host**: a settled, elected Cossack republic on the Don. Rich wheat land.
- **Ural Host**: guards the steppe gateway at Orenburg and taxes everything that passes.

#### Raiders
- **The Lost Legion**: deserters from every army stuck in Normandy.
- **The Ashen Riders**: scavengers from the Berlin fallout zone, resistant to radiation.
- **Po Valley Brigands**: militia-turned-bandits in the Italian north.
- **Carpathian Wolves**: mountain bands raiding Hungary and Galicia.

#### Communes and free states
- **Free Hanse** (Hamburg / Bremen / Lübeck): merchant democracy. Trades with America.
- **Lyon Commune**: former Resistance holding the fertile Rhône.
- **Ruhr Workers' Councils**: anarchist miners' councils.
- **Free Republic of Ossola**: the real 1944 partisan republic, survived. Mountain democracy on the Swiss border.
- **Free Brittany**: a coastal democracy kept alive by British and American ships.
- **Home Army Republic**: the Polish Underground State, out of the forests. Surrounded by enemies.
- **Balkan Partisan Federation**: Yugoslav partisans who never needed Moscow.
- **Holy League**: the Pope's church state in Rome. Relief agency, government and army in one.

### Siberia (east of the Urals)
The Soviet generals east of the Urals have carved out their own domains. The rest is empty taiga.
- **West Siberian Military District** (Omsk): a hardline Stalinist marshal who claims succession.
- **Yenisei Directorate** (Krasnoyarsk / Novosibirsk): a pragmatic junta that dropped communism and will deal with anyone, even Japan.
- **Transbaikal Front** (Irkutsk / Chita): the last Soviet army facing a real enemy, Japan, across the Amur.

## Geography of the wastes
| Zone | Where | What it's like |
|---|---|---|
| **Fallout zones** (irradiated) | Brandenburg, Mecklenburg and Saxony-Anhalt around **Berlin**. Upper Bavaria and Swabia around **Munich** | Nothing grows. Radiation sickness. Only the Ashen Riders go in willingly |
| **Dead cities** | Paris, the Rhineland, Vienna, Budapest, Belgrade, Moscow, Leningrad, Stalingrad, Kiev | Rubble fields full of scavengers |
| **Raider country** | The **old Normandy front** (minefields and wrecks), the **Pripyat marshes**, the **Drowned Lands** of Holland, burned **Silesia**, **East Prussia**, **Sicily** (mafia clans) | Ambushes, bandit lords and booby traps |
| **Quiet lands** | Burgundy, Aquitaine, Jutland, Poltava, the Great Hungarian Plain | Empty but safe farmland. The first places anyone can settle |
| **Empty taiga** | Siberia beyond the generals' domains | Vast and lawless |
